Vagamon is beautiful year-round, but seasons vary

Oct – May Peak

Peak Season

Clear skies, cool mountain air, and the iconic rolling mist over the tea estates. Perfect for walks and outdoor dining.

Jun – Sep Monsoon

Monsoon

Lush, dramatic, and quiet. The gardens are at their greenest. Roads can be wet — but the experience is truly serene.
